# Chalakudy experiment: where every number comes from

Generated by `python3 experiments/chalakudy_provenance.py` from `PARAMS` in `experiments/chalakudy.py`; edit those, not this file.

Kinds: **MEASURED** = taken from a cited source (for a literature model, the source is cited but nothing was measured in Chalakudy); **DERIVED** = computed from measured data by a script in `experiments/`; **ASSUMED** = a choice, with the reasoning given, swept where it could change the result.

## What is real data and what is not

- **Real (measured):** the town boundary; the position and footprint of every building (Overture: Google Open Buildings, dated 2023-05-01 in Overture, and OpenStreetMap); ground height (Copernicus DEM, a surface model); rivers and ponds (OpenStreetMap); open water on 24 Sentinel-1 radar images from January to September 2018; the event dates (heavy rain 15-17 Aug, dam releases 14-18 Aug, Chalakudy flooding on 15 or 17 Aug, last Thrissur substation back on 27 Aug).
- **Derived from it:** the study square; each building's ground estimate; flood-water maps and each building's distance to radar-visible flood water; the order in which devices fail (lowest ground first; in the study square no building is near radar-visible flood water, so ground height alone sets the order); network structure.
- **Assumed (no data):** how many devices take part and which buildings have them; how many buildings flood at the peak (swept 10-50%); the rise of the water between onset and 18 Aug; that flooded devices do not return; when the power was cut (pending KSEB); battery size and charge (placeholders); the radio's energy use (ns-3 defaults, not a 2018 phone); message traffic; the 150 m radio range (with a cited path-loss model as sensitivity); time compression of 1 day = 30 s.
